My name is Zwelibanzi. I'm a software engineer based in South Africa, currently completing a BSc in Mathematical and Computer Science—but the label I care about is engineer, not student.

When I meet a new technology I don't start with "How does this work?". I start with "What can I build with this?". Every project on this site is the answer to that question, one framework or idea at a time.

Most of my work lives on the backend: quiet, reliable systems that hold their shape as they grow. Lately I've been spending more time at the intersection of software and machine learning, particularly computer vision, where a well-placed model can make software feel almost alive.

Outside of the terminal I'm calm, easy to work with, and I take craft seriously. I'd rather ship one thoughtful thing than three careless ones.
