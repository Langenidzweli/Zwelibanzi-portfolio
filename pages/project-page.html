Virtual Guard — An AI-Powered Retail Surveillance Platform

Problem

Retail security teams monitor multiple CCTV cameras simultaneously, making it difficult to detect suspicious behaviour as it occurs. As a result, important incidents may be missed or identified too late.

Solution

Monitoring multiple CCTV cameras makes it difficult to detect suspicious behaviour in real time, increasing the risk of missed incidents.
